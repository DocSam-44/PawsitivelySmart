Why did I get into pet sitting??

I got into pet sitting by accident. I woman that lived near me was going away on holiday and needed someone to watch her cat and new kitten; I happily agreed. Since she also happens to be a dog groomer, I was lucky enough to be recommended to some of her clients with the sweetest little dogs. I love what I do and I love animals in general... If you don't believe me, maybe it will help to know I have a dog, a cat, a hamster, and two rats myself! I've always been told that if I love pets so much then I should become a vet, but I can't stand the idea of having to see them in their worst, most painful scenarios or even have to euthanize them, so now, I put my efforts towards helping them stay happy will their favourite humans are away.

Why did I get into pet sitting??

I got into tutoring after volunteering with the Learning Disabilities of Niagara Region (LDANR) Reading Rocks Program. The program was focussed on providing extra help for children who were struggling in school due to dyslexia. I found it really rewards to start from the beggining and help them learn new words, start recognizing old ones, and even request certain activities that they enjoyed. While developing new acitivities to keep the students engaged and motivated, I felt like I was making a difference in their lives. It's not fair that those who struggle with school are treated as not smart when sometimes it's just an issue of perspective. As such, when I was offered a tutoring job for the grandson of another woman I know, who needed help finishing strong in his last semester of highschool, and now in college, I was happy to help. And that brings us to today!
